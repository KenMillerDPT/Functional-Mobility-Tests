Cut-offs are screening references, not diagnoses: TUG ≥12 s (CDC STEADI); TUG-cog and TUG-manual are reported as dual-task cost against the standard TUG, with no cut-off; gait speed bands from Fritz & Lusardi 2009, with <1.0 m/s linked to higher risk of adverse outcomes; 5×STS >15 s for recurrent falls (Buatois 2008), age means from Bohannon 2006; 30s chair stand below-average scores from CDC STEADI; 2-minute step and 6-minute walk age ranges are the middle 50% of community-dwelling adults 60 to 94 (Rikli & Jones 1999, 6MWT converted from yards); 6MWT predicted distance and lower limit of normal from Enright & Sherrill 1998; FAB ≤25 of 40 (CSU Fullerton scoring form); FGA ≤22 of 30 (Wrisley & Kumar 2010); DGI under 19 of 24 predicts falls and above 22 marks safe ambulators; Berg Balance Scale under 45 of 56 indicates greater fall risk, with 41 to 56 low, 21 to 40 medium and 0 to 20 high (Berg 1992); FIST has no cut-off (Gorman 2010); Four Square Step Test over 15 s identifies multiple fallers (Dite & Temple 2002); ABC Scale bands under 50, 50 to 80 and over 80 percent with under 67 percent at risk for falling (Powell & Myers 1995; Lajoie & Gallagher 2004); FES-I bands 16 to 19, 20 to 27 and 28 to 64, and Short FES-I bands 7 to 8, 9 to 13 and 14 to 28 (Delbaere et al. 2010); not holding the tandem stance for 10 s on the 4-Stage Balance Test indicates increased fall risk (CDC STEADI); single limb stance under 5 s with eyes open is linked to higher risk of injurious falls (Vellas 1997), with age means of the best of 3 trials from Springer 2007; mCTSIB interpretation follows the Shirley Ryan AbilityLab form; Brief-BESTest has no single cut-off; occiput-to-wall distance above 0 cm is abnormal, with 4 cm or 5 cm used for hyperkyphosis; rib-pelvic distance of 2 fingerbreadths or less as a screen for vertebral fracture (Fukui cohort; National Osteoporosis Foundation materials say under 2); back scratch and chair sit-and-reach use the middle 50% by age and sex (Rikli & Jones 1999); mTBR percentile bands from Stanziano 2010. Confirm against your clinic's protocol.
